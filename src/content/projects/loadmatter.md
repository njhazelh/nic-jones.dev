LoadMatter reads your Strava activities and coaches you from them. Each morning it suggests the day's session (rest, recovery, easy, long or quality, or an easy ride as cross-training) with how long and why, and shows how the week is going against your plan.

The plan starts with showing up: a number of active days a week, any activity. Once that sticks for three weeks, the app offers a stress target for each week instead, filled however you like. Charts show fitness, fatigue and form over time, and running totals for each year side by side.

It's inspired by [Elevate](https://thomaschampagne.github.io/elevate/), the browser extension for Strava, and uses the published training-load formulas.
